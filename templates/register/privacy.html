Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Version 1.2 ReNote Media Platform
Summary in Plain English

At ReNote, your privacy is fundamental. We collect only the minimal data required to track your media, calculate your personal stats, and generate recommendations. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising trackers, and you can permanently erase all your data at any time via Settings.

1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

ReNote ("we", "us", or "our") is designed to be a personal, clutter-free media logging dashboard. We believe that privacy is not a luxury—it is a core feature. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is utilized, and the complete control you maintain over your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the following information when you create an account and interact with the service:

  • Account Identifiers: Your chosen username and email address, used exclusively for account authentication and password recovery.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashes: Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2 with SHA-256) before ever touching the database.
  • Profile Customization: Optional display name, biographical text, and your chosen preset avatar icon.
  • Library Logs & Activity: Titles added to your collection, media types (anime, manga, movie, novel), watch/read progress statuses, personal star ratings (1 to 5), repeat rewatch counts, and timestamps.
  • Essential Technical Data: Encrypted HTTP session cookies and CSRF protection tokens necessary to keep you securely signed in.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

To keep your footprint minimal and secure, ReNote explicitly does not collect:

  • No financial, billing, or payment card information (the platform is free to use).
  • No third-party ad tracking pixels or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of personal data with data brokers or marketing agencies.
  • No precise device geolocation tracking.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to power features you directly interact with:

  • Authenticating your account and validating your sign-in sessions.
  • Displaying your personalized library cards, status filters, and cover collections.
  • Computing real-time analytics such as media type percentages (Anime, Manga, Movie, Novel), top genres, and repeat rewatch tallies.
  • Fueling the "Pick for Me" randomizer to help you choose what to watch or read next.

5. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We employ robust technical and organizational security controls to safeguard your data:

  • All web traffic between your browser and ReNote is encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection is enforced across all state-changing forms and API actions.
  • Database credentials and application secrets are decoupled from application code and stored securely in environment variables.

6. Third-Party Media APIs

When you search for a title or browse catalogs, ReNote queries public metadata APIs (such as Jikan/MyAnimeList and TMDb) to fetch synopsis details, genres, and cover images. These catalog queries are performed server-side and never transmit your username, email, or personal identity to external third parties.

7. Your Rights & Data Autonomy (GDPR / CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, ReNote affords all users comprehensive data rights:

  • Right to Access: You can review your entire library, ratings, and profile information at any time directly through your dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your username, email, display name, bio, and avatar at any time in Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can permanently delete your account and all associated logs by visiting Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. When confirmed, all your data is instantly purged from our primary database with zero retention.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

ReNote uses only functional, first-party cookies necessary for user authentication and session security. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Updates to this Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect platform enhancements. When modifications occur, the effective date at the top of this document will be updated accordingly.

10. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via GitHub at github.com/venzblanz.
